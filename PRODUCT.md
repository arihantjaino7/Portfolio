## Users

Arihant Jain's portfolio visitors. Recruiters, collaborators, and curious developers are inferred audiences from the existing portfolio; no narrower audience was specified. Visitors need to understand his work, inspect projects, and contact him.

## Product Purpose

Present Arihant's real engineering work through a playable 2D Minecraft-inspired portfolio. Walking to and opening a chest reveals projects. Deliver a complete polished experience with sensible choices delegated by the user's explicit request to proceed autonomously in a new chat.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve factual identity, project descriptions, source and live links, resume, and existing case-study routes. Build on an isolated codex/ branch; do not replace production or merge automatically. Preserve the existing Astro static deployment and base-path support. Original code-native game art; no paid template purchase or asset copying. User explicitly forbids real-money spending: use free tooling and no paid APIs, purchases, or hosting upgrades. Keyboard and touch navigation, direct portfolio access, reduced motion, and readable fallback are required by the implementation brief.

## Brand Commitments

The user explicitly requested an extraordinary 2D game, with chests opening projects. Supplied reference screenshots show block-world atmosphere, inventory panels, hotbar navigation, and distinct biomes. They are references, not licensed assets.
